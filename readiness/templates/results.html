What this means for your revenue

  • AI shopping agents cannot recommend your products to anyone

  • "Find me a [your product]" queries return everyone except you
  • You won't see this in analytics — there's no bounce, no error, no traffic to measure

How to fix it

Your site's bot protection (likely Cloudflare, Akamai, or your CDN) is blocking requests with AI agent User-Agent strings. You need to allowlist these crawlers: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot. Check your CDN/WAF settings, or contact your hosting provider.
