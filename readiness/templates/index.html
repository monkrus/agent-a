FAQ

What's the difference between the free preview and a full scan?

The free preview runs {{ stats.check_counts.free }} structural checks — the same data AI agents would read (JSON-LD, robots.txt, price visibility, security, protocols). It gives you a score and headline findings. A full scan (via agency account) adds real AI shopping tests: an AI agent extracts price, availability, and product details across 10 visits, then opens a browser and attempts to add to cart, search for the product, navigate from the homepage, and reach checkout. You also get fix recipes, a revenue impact estimate, and step-by-step screenshots.

How is this different from Shopify's free scanner?

Shopify's scanner runs static checks — it looks at your schema markup and page structure. We do that too (for free). The full scan goes further: we send a real AI agent to the store and have it try to actually buy. Static checks tell you if the data is present. Our scan tells you if an AI agent can actually use it.

What stores do you support?

Optimized for Shopify. Most checks work on any platform including WooCommerce and BigCommerce. A few checks (like cart API) are Shopify-specific and show as inconclusive on other platforms.

How long does a full scan take?

The free preview takes 15–30 seconds. A full scan takes 2–4 minutes because it runs real AI extraction tests and browser purchase flows. Results stream live so you can watch in real time.

Can I white-label the reports?

Yes. Agency scans produce reports with your agency name instead of Agent-A branding. Share the link directly with your clients.

What's the ROI for agencies?

Each scan costs $10–$20 depending on your pack. A typical agency charges $1,500–$3,000 for the optimization work identified by the report. That's 60–150x return on the scan cost.
