What we check

01 Can agents read it? JSON-LD structured data, price in HTML, llms.txt, robots.txt, sitemap, page load time, and crawler access for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, and OAI-SearchBot. Free
02 Can agents extract correctly? A real AI shopper pulls price, availability, product name, and return policy multiple times — we grade accuracy and consistency across the runs. Deep Audit
03 Can agents act on it? Add-to-cart semantics, variant selectors, guest checkout, cart API, and checkout reachability — structural checks are free, browser walkthroughs are in the Deep Audit. Free + Deep Audit
04 Is it safe and trustworthy? Prompt injection in page content and reviews, cart rate limiting, checkout bot challenges, and exposed admin or API paths. Free
05 Does it hold up under traffic? Page load time against agent timeouts, and multi-user-agent blocking checked across four AI crawlers. Free
06 Does it speak agent protocols? MCP, OAuth, A2A, Auth.md, markdown negotiation, Link headers, DNS-AID, and Agent Skills / WebMCP / UCP / ACP. Free

FAQ

Is the scan really free?

Yes. The free scan runs 30 structural checks and gives you your readiness score, headline findings, and per-check pass/fail verdicts. The Deep Agent Audit ($49) sends a real AI shopper to your page and adds verified extraction results, evidence, and copy-paste fixes.

What stores do you support?

Currently optimized for Shopify. Most checks work on any platform, including WooCommerce and BigCommerce; Shopify-specific checks are clearly labeled.

Do you actually send AI agents to my page?

The free scan checks your page structure — the same data AI agents would read, without calling an LLM. The Deep Agent Audit ($49) sends a real Claude-powered shopping agent to your page multiple times, extracting price, availability, and policies, and grades accuracy and consistency across runs.

How long does a scan take?

The free scan typically takes 15–30 seconds (it makes ~20 HTTP requests to probe your site). The Deep Agent Audit takes 30 seconds to a minute. Results stream in live as each check completes.
