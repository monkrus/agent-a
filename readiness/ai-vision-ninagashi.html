04 — The verdict

Your site is better than most — with two gaps

The good news: ChatGPT and Gemini read your services, prices, contact info, and booking link correctly. Your site's structured data and page content are doing their job for the two biggest AI platforms. Most small businesses don't score this well.
Two things to fix:
Fix 1 — high impact

Make your prices visible to Perplexity

Perplexity can't read your prices because your site renders them with JavaScript. Adding prices to your structured data (Offer schema) or pre-rendering the services page would let every AI — including Perplexity — answer “how much does Nina charge?” instantly.

Fix 2 — high impact

Give each portfolio image a unique description

Right now every portfolio image says “COSMETIC TATTOO portfolio by Nina Gashi.” Change each one to describe the result: “Healed lip blush, natural pink, 3 weeks post-treatment” or “Lashline enhancement, subtle definition, no-makeup look.” This is the only way AI can show your actual work instead of other artists' photos.

Nice to have

Add client reviews

No ratings or reviews appear on your site. When AI compares you to competitors, it has no quality signal. Even a few testimonials with an aggregate rating would help.

Nice to have

Add booking action to structured data

ChatGPT found your Acuity link by crawling. Adding a ReserveAction to your structured data makes it instant and reliable across all platforms.
