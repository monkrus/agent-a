04 — What to fix

Four changes that make AI recommend you

High priority

Rewrite portfolio alt text

Each portfolio image needs a unique description of what it shows: “Healed lip blush, natural pink, 3 weeks post-treatment” instead of “COSMETIC TATTOO portfolio by Nina Gashi.” This is the single biggest reason AI shows other artists' photos.

High priority

Add all 18 services to structured data with prices

Your /services page has service names in structured data but no prices. Adding Offer schema with the dollar amount for each service means AI can answer “how much does Nina charge for freckles?” instantly — without reading the full page.

Medium priority

Add reviews to your site

No ratings or reviews appear on the site or in structured data. When AI compares you to competitors, it has no quality signal. Even 5–10 client testimonials with an aggregate rating would help.

Medium priority

Add booking action to structured data

AI found your Acuity link by crawling the page. Adding a ReserveAction to your structured data ensures every AI — not just ChatGPT — finds it on the first try.
